## Our recommended service

**PlayFlow Cloud** — a company we've had the pleasure of working with firsthand — is recommended as our top-pick for a hosting service. Their outstanding support, reliable service, and superior pricing have consistently impressed our staff and community.

PlayFlow Cloud features: Generous free tier with better pricing than competitors. Lobby & Matchmaking, with an included SDK handling the work. Worldwide dedicated and instanced servers. User-friendly control panel, metrics, support, and more.

## Other services

Here is a non-extensive selection of other hosting services that work well with FishNet.

### Dedicated servers

These hosts offer session-based servers, as well as persistent servers. Relay services may be available as well.

**PlayFlow Cloud**
- Pay-per-use pricing
- Session-based servers available
- Persistent servers available
- Relay servers available
- Match Making / Lobbies

**GameLift (AWS)**
- Pay-per-use pricing — a little more complex
- Session-based servers available
- Persistent servers available
- Relay servers available
- Match Making / Lobbies

> AWS is a bit more difficult to setup and manage. We generally only recommend AWS to experienced developers.
>
> We have a community guide for AWS available in our documentation.

### Relays only

These are relay-only services.

**Steam**

Steam relays are offered free when you launch your game on Steam; there is a percentage of sales, as well as an initial fee when using Steam.

We have an official FishNet transport for Steam, and there are third-party Unity assets available as well to help utilize Steam Relays. A popular asset is Toolkit for Steamworks Foundation by Heathen Engineering.

- Free for games launched using Steam
- Session-based relays
- Match Making / Lobbies
- Friends list (Steam)

**Epic Online Services (EOS)**

EOS provides a number of features for free. The API and documentation is a bit more complex, which may be difficult for beginners. A vetted third-party EOS transport is available for FishNet, which may help with some of those hurdles.

A cross-platform friends list is possible with EOS but at the cost of complexity; e.g.: you will not be able to directly access your Steam friends via the EOS platform. With this in mind, match making with friends may be more difficult.

- Free to all developers
- Session-based relays
- Match Making / Lobbies
- Friends list
